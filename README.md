# hittite_emotions
The “Akkadian and Hittite Emotions in Context (AHEC) – Towards a Lexicon of Emotions in Cuneiform Texts from Ancient Mesopotamia and Hittite Anatolia” is a collaboration between Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz and University of Tel Aviv, funded by DFG. <br>
Project members: Prof. Doris Prechel, Prof. Ulrike Steinert, Prof. Amir Gilan, Maria Molina, Letizia Savino

Hittite emotional terms can be divided into 18 macrodomains, corresponding in semantics with their Akkadian counterparts.<br>
For instruction on annotation of semantics of emotions see [Guidelines](https://github.com/mashenkeisraeli/hittite_emotions/blob/main/Guidelines.pdf).<br>
For full inventory of emotional lexemes in Hittite see [Hittite Emotions](https://github.com/mashenkeisraeli/hittite_emotions/blob/main/Hittite_emotions.csv).<br>

In the Hittite part of the project two directions of research are maintained:

The Database of Hittite Emotional Contexts (DHEC) - structured and lemmatized compound of paragraphs from Hittite texts containing emotional vocabulary<br>
The [Anatolian Emotions dataset](https://github.com/mashenkeisraeli/hittite_emotions/blob/etymology/Anatolian%20Emotions.csv) – etymology of the emotional lexicon in Anatolian languages (dataset)
